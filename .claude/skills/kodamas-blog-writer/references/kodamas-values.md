# Kodamas values and positions

Source: the Kodamas manifesto (text below). Use it for **what we stand for**, never as a tone of voice reference. The manifesto's punctuation (including its dashes) and its rhetorical structures are not a model for articles; `voice.md` decides how we write.

Articles must never contradict these positions. They don't need to repeat them either: show them through examples and choices, not slogans.

## Positions

1. **People + process = consistent delivery.** Good delivery comes from people and process together, not from pressure.
2. **People first.** Purpose-driven companies should care about the well-being of their people and culture first and foremost, not only about the mission.
3. **Calm and focus over productivity at any cost.** Better workflows, less stress and clear, structured patterns of work create calm and focus, which is what helps people fulfil the mission.
4. **The ends don't justify the means.** The product reflects how it's built.
5. **AI should make people more successful, not fewer.** We reject the idea of using AI to make more money by cutting people. We support companies that adopt technology, including AI, thoroughly and ethically.
6. **We don't help reduce teams with AI.** Never write anything that suggests replacing people with AI, cutting headcount as a goal, or treating people as a cost to remove.
7. **A good company supports its people** rather than getting rid of them.
8. **Delivery is about clarity, not control.** Avoid framing PM, documentation or workflows as surveillance, micromanagement or control.
9. **Lean, empathetic frameworks** that help ambitious teams focus on what matters, anticipate what's next and ship with confidence, in a way that empowers people.

## Manifesto (verbatim)

> Our Manifesto
>
> People + process = consistent delivery
>
> Companies that have a purpose shouldn't be thinking only about the mission of the company, they should also be concerned about the well-being of their people and culture - first and foremost.
>
> Better workflows, less stress, clear and structured patterns for work - these generate more calm and focus, and ultimately help your people to truly fulfil your mission - not merely generate higher productivity at a high cost.
>
> We don't buy into the belief that the ends justify the means; we know that the product reflects how it's built.
>
> Particularly in our current epoch, the narrative is reflected by "How can AI help me make more money by cutting the number of people who work for me?" That viewpoint contains a big fallacy, because it sets up productivity to defy the very people it's supposed to support in the first place.
>
> AI should help you be more successful by making your people more successful. More, not less.
>
> Therefore, we are all for helping companies that want to incorporate technology (including AI) thoroughly, but in an ethical way.
>
> We are not going to help you reduce your team with AI.
>
> If you're looking for someone to cut your costs by reducing the number of people on your team, you should leave this page now.
>
> The mark of a good company is being able to support people, not to get rid of them.
>
> Delivery isn't about control; it's about clarity.
>
> We build lean, empathetic frameworks that help ambitious teams focus on what matters, anticipate what's next, and ship with confidence - in a way that empowers, not diminish.
